Food Price Pressure in Malaysia

Geographic variation, rank ordering, and long-term trend in food and beverage prices

Author: Your Name | Date: May 2026

Why This Matters

Food price inflation affects household budgets differently across Malaysia's states. This story moves from where pressure is highest, to which states rank highest, to whether food prices have been building over time.

1. Where is pressure highest?

How to read this map: Darker states show stronger food price pressure. Use the year and month filters to compare how food inflation shifted across Malaysia over time. 2026 data only includes January to March.

2. Which states rank highest?

This chart ranks Malaysian states by food inflation for the selected month. The highlighted state shows where food price pressure was strongest compared with the same month in the previous year.

3. Has pressure increased over time?

Food prices have risen steadily across the selected states since 2020. The line chart helps compare whether food price increases are shared across states or concentrated in specific areas.

4. Which categories create pressure?

This heatmap compares price pressure across states and Consumer Price Index divisions. Darker red cells show stronger year-on-year inflation, helping identify whether food-related pressure is concentrated in specific states or shared across Malaysia.

5. When did food inflation become more intense?

Each vertical strip represents one month of food inflation. Darker orange-red periods show when food price pressure intensified, while blue periods show price easing compared with the same month in the previous year.

6. Is food inflation increasing or easing?

This slope chart compares food inflation between two selected periods. Red highlights the state with the strongest increase, while blue highlights the state with the strongest easing.

Data Sources & Methodology

Primary Data: Department of Statistics Malaysia CPI data for food and non-alcoholic beverages at state level.

Geographic Data: Malaysia state boundaries from TopoJSON for the map only.

Visualisation Idioms: Choropleth map, ranked lollipop chart, layered multi-line trend chart, state-versus-division heatmap barcode timeline, and slope chart.
