Food Price Pressure in Malaysia

An interactive view of where food prices are rising, how pressure shifts across states, and whether local production appears to ease affordability.

Author: Ahmad Sufi Bin Aziz Shah | Date: May 2026 | Tools: Vega-Lite, Pure.css

Why This Matters

Food price inflation affects household budgets differently across Malaysia's states. This story moves from where pressure is highest, to which states rank highest, to whether food prices have been building over time.

Key Questions

  • Which states face the strongest food price pressure?
  • How has food inflation changed across time?
  • Does local production appear to improve affordability?

Data Sources & Methodology

Primary Data

Department of Statistics Malaysia datasets accessed through OpenDOSM and data.gov.my, including Consumer Price Index, food prices, crop production, fish landings, and household price pressure indicators.

Geographic Data

Malaysia administrative state boundaries from geoBoundaries (https://www.geoboundaries.org/), with world-atlas background geography from the world-atlas package (https://github.com/topojson/world-atlas) for neighbouring country and ocean context.

Visualisation Idioms

Choropleth map, ranked lollipop chart, layered multi-line trend chart, state-versus-division heatmap, barcode timeline, slope chart, crop production map, production-versus-inflation bubble plot, fish landing connected dot plot, and a custom food security pressure profile strip.
